# OdoPocket Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 25 August 2026**

OdoPocket ("the app", "we", "our") is a mileage and expense tracking app for UK tax/expense claims.
This policy explains what data the app collects, why, where it's stored, and what choices you have.

## Summary

- Your journey, expense, and profile data is stored **only on your own device** — there is no
  OdoPocket server or cloud database. Nothing you enter is visible to us; we cannot see it.
- The app uses your **location** (including in the background while a tracking day is armed) to
  automatically detect and log business journeys for mileage claims.
- **Google Maps Platform** (Maps, Directions, Places, Geocoding) is used to show maps, resolve
  addresses, and calculate routes/distances — this sends location coordinates to Google as a
  normal part of using those services, governed by
  [Google's own Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- No data is sold. No advertising or analytics tracking is built into the app.

## What we collect, and why

### Location data
The app requests fine, coarse, and (for auto-tracking) background location access, plus activity
recognition (to detect when you're driving) and Bluetooth (to detect connecting to a vehicle). This
is used to:
- Automatically detect when a drive starts and ends, and record the route/distance as a mileage
  entry for you to review.
- Show your current position and journey routes on the in-app map.
- Resolve addresses and business names near a location (via Google Places/Geocoding).

Background location only runs while you've actively armed a tracking day in the app — it is never
running silently without your knowledge, and can be turned off entirely in favour of manual entry
at any time in Settings.

### Photos (camera)
The app can capture photos of receipts (for expense claims) and courier labels/serial numbers
(for the serial-scan feature). Photos are processed on-device (and via Google ML Kit for text
recognition) and stored locally with the associated expense/scan record.

### Profile and journey data you enter
Name, employee number, cost centre, email address, home address, vehicle details, customer/site
names, journey purposes, odometer readings, and expense amounts/categories — all typed or captured
by you, stored locally.

### Diagnostic logs
The app keeps a local, on-device log of auto-tracking activity (e.g. when a drive was detected,
connectivity/permission state) to help diagnose tracking issues. This log stays on your device
unless you explicitly choose to share/export it (e.g. to send to the developer for support), or
have turned on the optional "auto-send diagnostics when something looks wrong" setting, which is
off by default.

## Where your data lives

All journey, expense, receipt, and profile data is stored in a local database on your device. There
is currently no OdoPocket server and no cloud sync — we do not have access to your data, cannot see
it, and cannot recover it for you if your device is lost (you can create your own backup/export
file within the app).

## Third parties

- **Google Maps Platform** (Maps SDK, Directions API, Places API, Geocoding API, ML Kit) — receives
  location coordinates and, where relevant, photo content, to provide mapping, routing, address
  resolution, and text recognition. See
  [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- No other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are integrated into the app.

## Your choices

- **Auto-tracking is optional** — you can use the app in fully manual entry mode, in which case
  background location is never used.
- **Delete your data**: since everything is stored locally, uninstalling the app removes all
  locally stored data. [Account/data deletion path for a future account-based version to be added
  here once that exists.]
- **Diagnostics sharing** is opt-in and can be turned off in Settings > Diagnostics at any time.

## Children

OdoPocket is a business expense/mileage tool and is not directed at, or knowingly used by, children.
